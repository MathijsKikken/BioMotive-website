Mirjam Davidse is the project manager of the BioMotive project at the University of Twente. In this role, she coordinates the process around the installation of the new 0.5T upright MRI scanner at the UT.

Mirjam studied Technical Medicine at the University of Twente, completing her Master's degree in early 2026. With her background in both technology and medicine, she is interested in the opportunities that innovative medical technologies offer for research and healthcare. Through her role in the BioMotive project, she aims to contribute to the successful implementation of this new MRI facility and the research opportunities it will create.
