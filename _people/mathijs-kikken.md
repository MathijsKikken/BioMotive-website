Mathijs Kikken is a PhD candidate at UMC Utrecht with a background in Biomedical Engineering from Eindhoven University of Technology. His research focuses on MRI safety and the development of MRI methods to measure tissue temperature, with particular emphasis on heating caused by radiofrequency fields during scanning.

Alongside his research, Mathijs is the project manager for BioMotive at UMC Utrecht. He supports the coordination of the Utrecht team and works closely with colleagues at the University of Twente to align activities across both sites. He also contributes to the project’s communication, including the website and social media, helping to share developments within the consortium and with the wider research community.
