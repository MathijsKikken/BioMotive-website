Frank Simonis is the scientific lead of the BioMotive project in Twente, which is focused on the vertical 0.5T magnet system. On that lower field system weightbearing and upright scans can be done in realistic postures, and it can serve as a research environment for preparatory work on the walk-in-bore system of the UMC. He is assistant professor at the University of Twente, where his research is on imaging in functional postures with a focus on MRI physics, acquisition and reconstruction. Frank obtained a MSc degree in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Eindhoven followed by a PhD in MRI safety at the UMC Utrecht. In the BioMotive project his aim is to visualize anatomy during function in a realistic, daily life setting.
