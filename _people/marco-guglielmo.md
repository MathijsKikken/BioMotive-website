Marco Guglielmo is a cardiologist and radiologist working in the Departments of Cardiology and Radiology at UMC Utrecht, specializing in multimodality cardiovascular imaging. 
He enjoys working in multidisciplinary environments and collaborating with cardiologists, radiologists, researchers, and other healthcare professionals. He is particularly interested in bridging clinical practice, advanced imaging, research, and technological innovation.
His research focuses on advanced cardiac tissue characterization, atherosclerosis, and the development of more sustainable approaches to cardiac imaging.
Within BioMotive, he envisions a new generation of cardiovascular MRI capable of capturing the heart and circulation as dynamic systems rather than structures examined only at rest. He aims to enable researchers to study cardiovascular function and disease pushing imaging beyond the constraints of conventional static scanning.
