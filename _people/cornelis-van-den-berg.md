Professor Nico van den Berg leads the BioMotive project,
which brings together MRI technology and biomechanics to
study the human body during natural movement. As a professor
at UMC Utrecht, he contributes expertise in MRI physics,
hardware engineering and imaging methods to the development
of this new research infrastructure.

Nico studied Applied Physics at the University of Twente
and began his PhD at UMC Utrecht in 2002. His research
connects fundamental physical modelling with practical
innovations in MRI, drawing on mathematics, computing and
deep learning.

Within BioMotive, his ambition is to extend MRI beyond
conventional examinations performed while lying still,
enabling researchers to investigate how posture and movement
influence the internal functioning of the body.
