Dr Donnie Cameron leads the ‘Biomechanics of the musculoskeletal system’ use case of the BioMotive project. He is an Assistant Professor at Radboudumc with a strong interest in magnetic resonance imaging and spectroscopy of the musculoskeletal system. Donnie studied Physical Sciences and Medical Physics at the University of Aberdeen in Scotland, and completed his PhD there in 2013. He has worked in research centres in Switzerland, the United States, England, and the Netherlands, and his research straddles topics ranging from fundamental magnetic resonance methods development to application areas such as muscle ageing and neuromuscular disorders. Within BioMotive, Donnie will work to adapt technology prevalent in biomechanics laboratories—such as motion cameras, force plates, bicycle ergometers, and electromyography setups—to operate in situ in an open MRI environment.
