*Announcement published on 3 November 2025.*

![BioMotive consortium]({{ '/assets/images/20251007.jpg' | relative_url }})

*The BioMotive consortium.*

BioMotive has received a €20 million grant from the Dutch
Research Council (NWO) through the Large-Scale Research
Infrastructure programme.

The funding supports two complementary research facilities:
an open 0.5 tesla MRI scanner at the University of Twente
for upright imaging, and a planned 3 tesla walk-in MRI
scanner at UMC Utrecht for measurements during movement.

Together, these facilities will support research into
cardiovascular function, the musculoskeletal system and
metabolism. The infrastructure is intended for scientific
research.
