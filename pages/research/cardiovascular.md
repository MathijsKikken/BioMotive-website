The heart and blood vessels continuously adapt to the demands of the
body. BioMotive aims to investigate how movement, posture and physical
effort influence cardiovascular function.

## Research focus

By developing MRI methods for studying the body under more natural
conditions, we aim to gain insight into heart mechanics and blood flow.
Potential applications include heart failure and hereditary
cardiovascular conditions.
