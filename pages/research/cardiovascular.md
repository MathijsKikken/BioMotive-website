The heart and blood vessels respond continuously to changes in posture
and physical activity. BioMotive aims to combine MRI with
cardiopulmonary exercise testing to study cardiac function and blood
flow during upright exercise.

## Planned use cases

- **Heart failure:** investigating cardiac function and exercise
  limitations, including in patients being assessed for advanced therapies.
- **Athletes:** studying adaptations to training and distinguishing
  these from cardiovascular disease.
- **Cardiomyopathies:** investigating how heart muscle disease affects
  cardiac performance during exercise.
- **Pulmonary hypertension:** studying the response of the heart and
  pulmonary circulation to physical effort.
